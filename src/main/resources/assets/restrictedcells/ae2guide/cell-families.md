&nbsp;

Every AE2 cell size from 1k to 256k has a Restricted item and fluid version, including the portable cells.

Other mods add more cells:

*   **MEGA Cells** adds Restricted 1M to 256M MEGA item and fluid cells, including the portable cells.
*   **Applied Mekanistics** adds Restricted chemical cells from 1k to 256k, standard and portable. With MEGA Cells installed too, it also adds the 1M to 256M chemical cells. Restricted chemical cells refuse radioactive chemicals, like the originals.
*   **Expanded AE** adds Restricted ME Dual Storage Cells, which store items and fluids, and Restricted Artificial
    Universe item and fluid cells.

Without those mods, their Restricted Cells do not exist. All other cells keep working.

Every Restricted Cell uses the same restrictions screen.
