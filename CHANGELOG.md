# Changelog

## [0.1.0]

- Restricted cells with a cap on total resources and a cap on types, set in the Cell Workbench
- Per-type resource cap with an Equal Distribution Card
- The amount field accepts the suffixes k, M, G and T
- Item and fluid cells from 1k to 256k, standard and portable
- MEGA Cells, Expanded AE and Applied Mekanistics support
- Recipes: the normal cell plus a Logic Processor. Sneak and right-click turns a cell back
- In-game guide and German translation
